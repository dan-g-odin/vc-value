# Code of conduct

This project is a shared, evidence-based resource. We ask everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith;
- argue from evidence: cite sources, show arithmetic and accept correction;
- keep discussion about the data and the method, not about people;
- not post personal information about private individuals, or confidential material;
- not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the contact details on the repository profile.

These guidelines draw on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
